LISA Facts

✦Her Swiss stepfather, Marco Brüschweiler, is a top certified chef in Thailand.
✦Her birth name was Pranpriya. She legalized it to Lalisa (meaning "the praised one") after a fortune telling.
✦Childhood friends with GOT7's BamBam, performing together in the dance crew We Zaa Cool.
✦The only applicant accepted during YG's 2010 Thailand Auditions. Trained for 5 years and 3 months.
✦Speaks fluent Thai, English, Korean, Japanese, and basic Chinese.
✦Voted by the members and staff as the person who acts most differently on stage vs off stage (extremely playful and mischievous in private).
✦Voted as the best at aegyo in BLACKPINK. Referred to as the "Thailand Princess".
✦Appeared in G-Dragon's labelmate Taeyang's 'Ringa Linga' music video.
✦Loves make-up and can play the Ukulele and the guitar.
✦She is Buddhist. Her favorite foods are French fries, sushi, and Korean Gamjatang.
✦Her favorite color is Yellow, and her favorite number is 27 (her birthday).
✦Has five cats (Leo, Luca, Lily, Louis, Lego) and an adopted dog named Love.
✦Parris Goebel described Lisa as a dancer who is naturally blessed rather than made.
✦Nicknamed "LaLi Con Artist" because of her cheating habits during board games.
✦Avid photographer; loves capturing pictures of other members.
✦Close friends with CLC's Sorn, NCT's Ten, (G)I-DLE's Minnie, Twice's Mina & Tzuyu, and GOT7's Jackson.

✦Is the number one most-followed K-pop idol on Instagram globally.
