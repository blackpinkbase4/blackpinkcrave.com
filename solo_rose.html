ROSÉ is the main vocalist and lead dancer, recognized worldwide for her unique, airy voice. Born in New Zealand and raised in Australia, she brings a distinct international flair to the group. Her solo debut with ON THE GROUND and her studio album projects have topped global music charts. She is highly skilled at playing the guitar and piano, often adding acoustic elements to her performances. Representing brands like Saint Laurent and Tiffany & Co., she is celebrated for her rock-chic style. Her emotional delivery and artistic depth define her as a powerful modern musician.
