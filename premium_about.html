About the Platform

Blackpink Crave is your ultimate fan-driven command center. Learn about our vision, goals, features, and milestones supporting the girls.

The Ultimate Hangout

Welcome to your ultimate 24/7 hangout and tracking hub for BLACKPINK and the girls! Whether you are a proud BLINK, a dedicated solo stan, or just casually loving their music, we are so glad you found us.

This site was built by fans, for fans. We know how fast the K-pop world moves and how massive the individual solo projects are. That is why we created one cozy corner on the internet where everyone can find exactly what they need to support Jisoo, Jennie, Rosé, and Lisa without any drama.

Why We Are Here 🥰

Being a fan is way more fun when we do it together. Blackpink Crave is here to bring everyone together—no matter which member brought you here. We want to make it super easy to stay connected, stream smart, and show the world our collective power.

Thank you for stopping by! Grab your lightstick, look around, and let’s keep making history with our favorite girls.
