August 2016

Square One

'BOOMBAYAH' / 'Whistle' — Achievements: Earned a "Perfect All-Kill" on Korean charts with "Whistle" and became the fastest girl group to win #1 on a music show (SBS Inkigayo) just 13 days after debut.

November 2016

Square Two

'Playing with Fire' / 'Stay' — Achievements: "Playing with Fire" topped the Billboard World Digital Songs chart and dominated local music programs like M Countdown.

June 2017

"As If It's Your Last"

Achievements: Reached #1 on the Billboard World Digital Songs chart and was performed at the SBS Gayo Daejeon, featuring their famous uniform stage outfits.

June 2018

Square Up

'DDU-DU DDU-DU' — Achievements: Broke records with the highest 24-hour YouTube debut for a K-pop group at the time and kicked off their massive In Your Area World Tour.

April 2019

Kill This Love

Coachella Stage: Made history as the first-ever K-pop girl group to perform at Coachella (Sahara Stage), delivering a legendary live band set that broke them into the US mainstream.

June 2020

"How You Like That"

Achievements: Broke multiple Guinness World Records, including the biggest YouTube Premiere in history with 86.3 million views in 24 hours.

August 2020

"Ice Cream"

(with Selena Gomez) — Achievements: Debuted at #13 on the Billboard Hot 100, marking their highest entry on the chart at the time.

October 2020

The Album

'Lovesick Girls' — Achievements: Became the first K-pop girl group album to sell over 1 million copies, peaking at #2 on the Billboard 200.

August 2022

"Pink Venom"

Stages/Achievements: Became their first song to top the Billboard Global 200. They later performed it live at the 2022 MTV Video Music Awards (VMAs).

September 2022

Born Pink

'Shut Down' — Coachella Stage: Led into making history as the first Asian and first K-pop act to headline Coachella (Main Stage) in April 2023, performing in custom hanboks for a crowd of 125,000.

August 2023

"The Girls"

Achievements: Released as an exclusive soundtrack for BLACKPINK The Game, winning Best Metaverse Performance at the MTV VMAs.

July 2025

"JUMP"

Stages/Achievements: First premiered live on stage during the opening night of the Deadline World Tour at Goyang Stadium. It reached #28 on the Billboard Hot 100.

February 2026

Deadline EP

Achievements: Their 3rd mini-album capped off their global stadium tour, with Pitchfork highlighting the track as a powerful evolution of their classic "BLACKPINK in your area" identity.
